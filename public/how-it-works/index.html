How TeamWheels works

Install in Teams

Add TeamWheels from AppSource to your Microsoft Teams in just a few clicks.

Offer or find rides

Use the bot to declare your commute or search for available carpools nearby.

Connect & confirm

Request to join a carpool. The driver approves, and you’re all set to ride together.

Monitor CO₂ savings and carpooling stats through the admin dashboard.

TeamWheels works inside Microsoft Teams

Our application has been validated by Microsoft and is available on AppSource. It’s easy to install and use for all your employees.

A bot that meets your needs

Our bot is here to help you organize your trips, find carpoolers, and get more information about your commutes.

Declare your trips in one click

The driver declares their trip using predefined bot commands. They specify the date/time of the trip, the departure and arrival points, and the number of available seats.

Search for available carpools

Employees can search for available carpools based on their departure location, destination, and departure time.

Submit a carpool request

Employees can submit a carpool request for a specific trip.

Carpool request validation

The driver receives a notification to approve or decline the carpool request. Once approved, both employees receive a confirmation notification.

Departure time

The bot reminds carpool participants when it’s time to leave by sending a notification and a message in the chat.
The driver can reschedule the carpool by replying to the bot’s message.

A dashboard for administrators

Get an overview of TeamWheels usage in your company without compromising your employees’ privacy.
