## How the demo works

The form above triggers a reply within one business day. The demo itself is a **30-minute video call** in three parts: your context (sites, headcount, parking, Scope 3 or commuter-benefit goals), a complete walkthrough inside Microsoft Teams — an employee declaring a commute, matching, notifications, the administrator dashboard, the export of trip records for commuter benefits — and the scoping of a free 30-day trial on a pilot group. You can also start with the [60-second demo video](/en/demo/).

## Who it is for

TeamWheels is built for organisations on Microsoft 365 that want employees sharing rides without an IT project: **HR and benefits teams**, **mobility and facilities managers**, **sustainability teams** in charge of Scope 3 reporting, and **IT leaders** who sign off on tenant deployments. Campuses, hospitals and multi-employer business parks are pointed to the [white-label carpool platform](/en/white-label-carpool-platform/).

## What to prepare

- The **number of employees** in scope and the share who drive alone;
- Your **sites** (address, headcount, dominant shifts);
- Confirmation that a **Microsoft Teams administrator** can install an app from AppSource;
- Your priorities: parking, Scope 3, commuter benefits, employer brand.

Don't have those numbers yet? The [carpooling savings calculator](/en/blog/carpooling-savings-calculator-corporate-co2-roi-2026/) gives a first estimate in two minutes, and the [corporate carpooling software](/en/corporate-carpooling-software/) page details how it works and what it costs.
