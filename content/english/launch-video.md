## What the launch video shows

1. **The problem** — colleagues work in the same office and drive the same route, each alone in their car.
2. **Just ask** — an employee types "Find me a ride to Lyon, Tuesday 8am" to TeamWheels in Microsoft Teams.
3. **Instant matching** — the agent answers with the colleagues who match the route and departure time, and the employee joins a ride in one click.
4. **Made for commutes and team offsites** — fewer cars, lower costs and less CO₂, with no extra app to install.

For the full product walkthrough (ride matching, the conversational bot and the Scope 3 dashboard), watch the [60-second demo](/en/demo/), or read more about the [corporate carpooling software](/en/corporate-carpooling-software/).
