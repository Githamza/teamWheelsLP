**In short:** Oxycar, a French employee carpooling platform based in Alsace, was placed in judicial liquidation in May 2023 and the proceedings were closed in November 2023. If your company or business park used Oxycar, or you are looking for the same kind of tool today, this page compares Oxycar with TeamWheels and explains how to relaunch a carpool program in a few weeks.

## What happened to Oxycar {#oxycar}

The public facts, with their sources:

- **Business**: Oxycar, founded in 2017 in Rosheim (Bas-Rhin, France), offered an employer-funded carpooling platform for employees, integrated with the Microsoft environment ([Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/oxycar-mise-sur-le-covoiturage-entre-salaries-finance-par-lentreprise-733467), in French).
- **16 May 2023**: the judicial court of Saverne orders the simplified judicial liquidation of Oxycar SAS, SIREN 833 523 707 (BODACC A no. 20230107, notice 3541; [Pappers record](https://www.pappers.fr/entreprise/oxycar-833523707)).
- **14 November 2023**: the proceedings are closed for insufficient assets (BODACC A no. 20230227, notice 2501).
- **The tool**: according to [Le Journal des Entreprises](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/breve/la-start-up-de-covoiturage-oxycar-liquidee-2064113), the carpooling tool was not taken over.

## Switch to TeamWheels in four steps {#migration}

1. **Install (5 minutes).** A Microsoft 365 admin adds TeamWheels from the Microsoft Teams Store and grants Entra ID consent. Employees have no account to create: your directory becomes the carpooler list.
2. **Set up your sites and rules.** Site addresses, working hours, matching rules and, in France, your Forfait Mobilités Durables amount.
3. **Run a pilot (2 to 4 weeks).** 20 to 50 volunteers, ideally former Oxycar users who already know the idea and can act as ambassadors.
4. **Roll out.** Announce it to all employees in Teams. Monthly records start with the first shared trips.

## When TeamWheels is not the right choice {#limits}

- **You are not on Microsoft 365**: TeamWheels runs in Teams, so another solution will suit you better.
- **Your employees mainly need an open network of carpoolers** outside your organization: a consumer app has the advantage of volume.
- **Few employees on your site come from the same area**: with no coworker on the route, there is nobody to match.
