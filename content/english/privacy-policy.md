TeamWheels ("**TeamWheels**", "**we**", "**us**") provides an employee carpooling
and commute management platform delivered inside Microsoft Teams. This Privacy
Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it,
and the rights you have over it.

It covers two different situations:

* **This website** (`teamwheelsapp.com`) — marketing pages, blog, demo requests
  and contact forms. Here TeamWheels acts as the **data controller**.
* **The TeamWheels platform** — the carpooling service your employer deploys in
  Microsoft Teams. Here your **employer is the data controller** and TeamWheels
  acts as a **data processor** on their instructions, under a data processing
  agreement. If you are an employee using the platform, your employer's own
  privacy notice applies first; contact them to exercise your rights.

## 1. Who we are

TeamWheels operates the TeamWheels employee carpooling platform and this
website. For any question about this policy or about your personal data, write
to us at **[contact@teamwheels.fr](mailto:contact@teamwheels.fr)**.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 When you use this website

| Data | Where it comes from | Why we collect it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, business email, company, job title, phone number, message | Demo request and contact forms you fill in | To answer your request, schedule a demo, and follow up commercially |
| IP address, approximate country/region | Automatic, via IP geolocation | To show prices and content relevant to your region, and to protect the site against abuse |
| Pages viewed, referrer, device and browser type, session behaviour | Automatic, via analytics cookies and similar technologies | To measure audience, improve the site, and measure our advertising |
| Chat messages | Our live-chat widget, if you start a conversation | To answer your questions |

We do **not** ask for special categories of data (health, religion, political
opinions, etc.) and ask you not to include them in free-text fields.

### 2.2 When your employer deploys the TeamWheels platform

Processed on behalf of your employer, typically:

* **Identity and workplace data** from Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) — name,
  professional email address, employee identifier, work site, and team.
* **Commute data** — home area or pickup point, usual commute schedule, offered
  or requested trips, matches, and confirmed journeys.
* **Program data** — participation, distances travelled, estimated CO₂ savings,
  and any mileage or commuter-benefit information your employer chooses to track.
* **Technical logs** — connection timestamps and events needed for security and
  troubleshooting.

Reporting delivered to your employer's administrators is **aggregated** so that
it measures the program, not individual employees.

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |

| Replying to demo requests and contact messages | Steps taken at your request prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

Strictly necessary cookies keep the site working (language preference, consent
choice, security). They are always active.

Measurement and advertising technologies are loaded only in line with your
consent choice, and include:

* **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Tag Manager** — audience measurement.
* **Google Ads** — conversion measurement for our campaigns.
* **Microsoft Advertising (UET)** and the **LinkedIn Insight Tag** — advertising
  measurement and audiences. Advertising storage is set to *denied* by default
  and upgraded only after you accept.
* **Microsoft Clarity** — anonymised session statistics used to improve page
  usability.
* **Crisp** — the live-chat widget.
* **IP geolocation (ipapi.co)** — approximate country detection to localise
  content.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies for this site in your
browser and making a new choice, and you can block cookies entirely in your
browser settings. Blocking non-essential cookies does not affect access to the
site.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

* **Service providers (sub-processors)** acting on our instructions — cloud
  hosting, Microsoft 365/Entra ID, form handling (Web3Forms), email delivery,
  analytics and advertising providers listed above, and our CRM.
* **Your employer**, for platform data, as the controller of that data.
* **Professional advisers, auditors, or authorities**, where we are legally
  required to do so.

An up-to-date list of sub-processors used for the platform is available to
customers on request at
[contact@teamwheels.fr](mailto:contact@teamwheels.fr).

## 6. International transfers

Platform data is hosted in the European Union. Some of our providers
(analytics and advertising in particular) may process data outside the EU/EEA.
Where that happens, transfers are covered by an adequacy decision or by the
European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with additional
safeguards.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Demo and contact requests | 3 years from our last contact with you |
| Prospect and customer relationship records | Duration of the relationship, then 3 years |
| Platform data processed for your employer | For the term of their subscription; deleted or returned within 30 days of termination, unless a longer period is legally required |
| Analytics data | Up to 14 months |
| Accounting records | 10 years, as legally required |
| Security and technical logs | 12 months |

## 8. Security

TeamWheels is built to enterprise security requirements. Measures include
authentication through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) single sign-on — so no
separate password is created for TeamWheels — encryption of data in transit,
strict role-based access control, hosting with certified providers, logging of
administrative actions, and privacy-by-design defaults such as aggregated
reporting and approximate pickup areas rather than precise home addresses.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, erase it,
restrict or object to its processing, obtain it in a portable format, withdraw
consent at any time, and set instructions for the fate of your data after death.

To exercise these rights for data we control, email
**[contact@teamwheels.fr](mailto:contact@teamwheels.fr)**. We respond within one
month. For data processed on behalf of your employer, contact your employer
directly; if you contact us, we will forward the request to them.

If you believe your data protection rights have not been respected, you may
lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the
**CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## 10. Children

TeamWheels is a business service intended for employees of our corporate
customers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 16.
