TeamWheels ("**TeamWheels**", "**we**", "**us**") provides an employee carpooling
and commute management app delivered inside Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365
Copilot. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we
collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

It covers two different situations:

* **This website** (`teamwheelsapp.com`) — marketing pages, blog, demo requests
  and contact forms. Here TeamWheels acts as the **data controller**.
* **The TeamWheels app** — the carpooling service your organization installs in
  Microsoft Teams. Here your **employer is the data controller** and TeamWheels
  acts as a **data processor** on their instructions, under a data processing
  agreement. If you are an employee using the app, your employer's own privacy
  notice applies first; contact them to exercise your rights. Section 3
  describes the app in full.

## 3. The TeamWheels app for Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot

This section describes how the **TeamWheels** application ("the app"), published
by Hmz-digital, processes personal data when your organization installs it from
the Microsoft marketplace.

**What the app collects.** When you use TeamWheels, we process: your display
name and Microsoft Teams user identifiers (including your Microsoft Entra
object ID and tenant ID); the trip details you enter (departure and destination
places, dates, times, seats); your carpool interactions (join requests,
acceptances, cancellations); conversation references needed to send you
notifications; your language preference; and any feedback you submit on the
app's replies (stored with a hashed user identifier). The app does not access
your calendar, files, email, or contacts.

**Where it is stored.** All app data is stored in Microsoft Azure (Azure Table
Storage) in the **France Central** region, in the European Union, encrypted at
rest and in transit.

**AI processing.** TeamWheels is an AI-assisted agent. To understand your
messages, the conversation context is processed by **Azure OpenAI Service**
within our Azure subscription. Your messages are not used to train AI models.
AI-generated replies are labeled as such in the app, and no trip is ever
created, changed, or cancelled without your explicit confirmation via a button.

**Processors.** We rely solely on Microsoft Azure services (hosting, storage,
Azure OpenAI, Azure Maps for place lookups). We do not sell personal data, use
it for advertising, or share it with any other third party.

**Retention and erasure.** App data is retained while the app remains installed
in your organization. You or your administrator can request deletion of all
data related to a user or tenant at any time by contacting
**[hamza@teamwheelsapp.com](mailto:hamza@teamwheelsapp.com)**; erasure is
performed within 30 days. Data breaches are notified in accordance with GDPR
Article 33 (within 72 hours of confirmation).

**Contact.** For any privacy question or request regarding the TeamWheels app:
[hamza@teamwheelsapp.com](mailto:hamza@teamwheelsapp.com).

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

This section concerns **this website only**. The app does not use advertising or
analytics cookies.

Strictly necessary cookies keep the site working (language preference, consent
choice, security). They are always active.

Measurement and advertising technologies are loaded only in line with your
consent choice, and include:

* **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Tag Manager** — audience measurement.
* **Google Ads** — conversion measurement for our campaigns.
* **Microsoft Advertising (UET)** and the **LinkedIn Insight Tag** — advertising
  measurement and audiences. Advertising storage is set to *denied* by default
  and upgraded only after you accept.
* **Microsoft Clarity** — anonymised session statistics used to improve page
  usability.
* **Crisp** — the live-chat widget.
* **IP geolocation (ipapi.co)** — approximate country detection to localise
  content.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies for this site in your
browser and making a new choice, and you can block cookies entirely in your
browser settings. Blocking non-essential cookies does not affect access to the
site.

## 6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

* **Microsoft Azure**, the sole set of services processing app data (hosting,
  Azure Table Storage, Azure OpenAI, Azure Maps), as described in section 3.
* **Website service providers** acting on our instructions — form handling
  (Web3Forms), email delivery, the analytics and advertising providers listed
  above, and our CRM.
* **Your employer**, for app data, as the controller of that data.
* **Professional advisers, auditors, or authorities**, where we are legally
  required to do so.

## 7. International transfers

App data is stored in the European Union (Microsoft Azure, France Central).
Some of the website providers listed above (analytics and advertising in
particular) may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where that happens, transfers
are covered by an adequacy decision or by the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses together with additional safeguards.

## 9. Security

Measures include authentication through Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) — so no
separate password is created for TeamWheels — encryption of app data at rest
and in transit, storage inside a European Azure region, strict access control,
and data minimisation: the app requests no access to your calendar, files,
email, or contacts. Data breaches are notified in accordance with GDPR Article
33, within 72 hours of confirmation.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, erase it,
restrict or object to its processing, obtain it in a portable format, withdraw
consent at any time, and set instructions for the fate of your data after death.

To exercise these rights for data we control, email
**[contact@teamwheels.fr](mailto:contact@teamwheels.fr)** for the website, or
**[hamza@teamwheelsapp.com](mailto:hamza@teamwheelsapp.com)** for the app. We
respond within one month. For app data processed on behalf of your employer,
you may also contact your employer directly; if you contact us, we will forward
the request to them.

If you believe your data protection rights have not been respected, you may
lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority — in France, the
**CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
