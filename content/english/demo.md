## What the demo covers

The video above compresses an employee's and an administrator's journey into one minute. The personalised demo takes about thirty minutes on a video call and follows the same thread, with your sites and constraints:

1. **The bot in Microsoft Teams** — an employee declares their usual commute (home area, site, hours) by answering a few questions, then offers a seat or asks for one. No app to download, no account to create: sign-in runs through Entra ID.
2. **Matching and confirmation** — the algorithm proposes compatible colleagues by route, schedule and site; the driver confirms and passengers get a Teams notification when it is time to leave. We also show how to flag a day off or change hours in a single message.
3. **The administrator dashboard** — active carpoolers, trips shared, kilometres avoided, CO₂ saved, and the monthly export of trip records for commuter-benefit programmes (IRS Section 132(f), UK schemes, France's forfait mobilités durables).
4. **Scoping the trial** — pilot scope, sites to declare, rollout calendar, and answers for your IT and HR teams.

## Who the demo is for

- **HR and benefits teams** — to assess real adoption, commuter-benefit administration and the effect on retention.
- **Mobility and facilities managers** — to quantify parking spaces freed and multi-site organisation.
- **Sustainability and ESG teams** — to check the quality of the Scope 3 Category 7 data exported to GHG Protocol and CSRD reporting.
- **IT and security** — to validate deployment in your Microsoft 365 tenant, Entra ID SSO and data processing.

Three people are usually enough: an HR or mobility sponsor, a Teams administrator and, ideally, a future programme ambassador.

## What to prepare

- The **number of employees** in scope and the share who drive alone.
- Your list of **sites** (address, headcount, dominant shifts) and any multi-employer locations.
- Confirmation that your teams work on **Microsoft 365** and that a Teams administrator can install an app from AppSource.
- Your priorities: parking, Scope 3, commuter benefits, employer brand.

With those elements we propose a pilot scope and a quantified estimate at the end of the call.
