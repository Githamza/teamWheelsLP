## Why a Carpooling ROI Calculator Matters in 2026

Employee commuting is now one of the most scrutinised categories in corporate sustainability reporting. Three forces have pushed carpooling ROI to the top of the HR and ESG agenda:

- **Scope 3 disclosure is going mainstream.** The EU's CSRD, the UK's climate-related disclosure rules and the SEC climate rule in the US are all pushing companies to quantify — not estimate — employee commuting emissions.
- **Fuel and parking costs keep climbing.** Diesel and petrol prices are still near record highs across Europe, and urban parking rates have doubled in many city-centre offices since 2020.
- **Talent retention is commute-sensitive.** Nearly 1 in 4 employees has already quit a job because of a bad commute, making mobility benefits a measurable retention lever.

Without hard numbers, carpooling remains a "nice-to-have." With a calculator that translates fleet, commute and fuel assumptions into **tonnes of CO₂, euros saved per driver, parking spots freed and an ESG score**, the conversation shifts from *if* to *when*.

## Methodology: Why the Numbers Are Defensible

The calculator is intentionally transparent. It is based on:

- **ADEME Base Empreinte** emission factors for France and the UK (gCO₂e per km, per fuel type).
- **US EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub** for the United States (grams of CO₂ per vehicle-mile).
- Fuel prices and currencies aligned with the country selected.
- A **conservative average occupancy of 2.2 passengers per carpool**, in line with ADEME's 2023 mobility observatory.
- Parking-spot reductions proportional to the share of trips converted from solo to shared.

The underlying logic matches the **GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard**, Category 7 (Employee Commuting), so the outputs can be integrated directly into CSRD, CDP or CDP Supply Chain disclosures.
