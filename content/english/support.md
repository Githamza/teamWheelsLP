This page is for anyone using or evaluating the TeamWheels app for Microsoft
Teams and Microsoft 365 Copilot. You do not need an account or a login to reach
us. If you are looking for a product demo instead, see the
[contact page](/en/contact/).

## Contact support

**Email:** [hamza@teamwheelsapp.com](mailto:hamza@teamwheelsapp.com)

We reply within **one business day** (Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Paris
time). Support is available in **English and French**.

To help us answer quickly, include:

* your organization name and the Microsoft Teams client you use (desktop, web,
  iOS or Android);
* what you were trying to do and what happened instead;
* a screenshot of the card or message, if you have one;
* the approximate date and time (with your time zone).

Please do not send passwords or other secrets by email. We never ask for them.

## Help inside the app

TeamWheels answers most questions directly in the chat. Open the TeamWheels
conversation in Teams and type **help** (or **aide** in French) to see what the
app can do and the phrases it understands. The **Help** card also has a
**Support** button that brings you to this page.

You can also use the commands listed in the message box:

* **Offer a ride** — announce a carpool you are driving.
* **Find a carpool** — search upcoming rides on your route and date.
* **List my trips** — see the carpools you drive or have joined.
* **Carpool stats** — see your company's numbers for the last 30 days.

## Common questions

**I cannot find TeamWheels in Teams.**
The app is installed from the Teams app store (AppSource). Your IT
administrator may need to approve it first. If it is blocked, ask your Teams
administrator to allow the "TeamWheels" app in the Teams admin center.

**Do I need a TeamWheels account?**
No. TeamWheels uses your existing Microsoft Teams identity. There is no
separate sign-up and no password to manage.

**No ride matched my search.**
Ride search returns trips on the **same calendar date** within **25 km** of the
requested origin and destination. Try a nearby town or another date, or offer
the ride yourself so colleagues can join you.

**I did not receive a reminder or a notification.**
Reminders are sent 24 hours and 1 hour before departure, in the chat and in the
Teams activity feed. Check that notifications for TeamWheels are enabled in
Teams settings, and that the trip is still confirmed. Reminder timing is not
configurable yet.

**The app created, changed or cancelled something I did not want.**
TeamWheels only creates, changes or cancels a trip when you confirm with a
button on a card. If you believe something happened without your confirmation,
email us with the trip details and we will investigate.

**Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?**
Trip data is stored in your organization's Azure region. Your employer is the
data controller for the app. To access or erase your data, contact your
employer's Teams administrator or email us. Details are in the
[privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/).

## For IT administrators

* **Installation:** deploy TeamWheels from the Teams admin center or let users
  install it from the Teams app store.
* **Permissions:** the app requests *identity* and *messageTeamMembers*, plus
  the resource-specific permission *TeamsActivity.Send.User* to post reminders
  to the activity feed.
* **Domains:** the app calls `bot.teamwheelsapp.com` only.
* **Removal:** uninstalling the app for your tenant stops all processing. Email
  us to request deletion of stored trip data.

For contractual questions (subscription agreement, order form, data processing
agreement) or a demo, write to
[contact@teamwheels.fr](mailto:contact@teamwheels.fr) or use the
[contact page](/en/contact/).

## Report a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in TeamWheels, email
[hamza@teamwheelsapp.com](mailto:hamza@teamwheelsapp.com) with "Security" in
the subject line. We acknowledge reports within one business day.
