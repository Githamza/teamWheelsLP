# TeamWheels B2B prospects — FR / CH / UK (2026-08-30)

Source: Google Maps sweep (OpenSEO `search_local_businesses`, categories manufacturer + logistics_service, min 10 reviews) across six high-density driver-commute employment zones. Filtered to sites matching the ICP: large driver-heavy workforces, single site, out-of-center location, shift patterns, parking pressure. Parcel shops, showrooms and small workshops removed.

Pitch angle per segment:
- **3PL / warehousing**: shift workers, no transit at 5am, recruitment radius ("carpooling widens your labor pool"), RSE/CSRD reporting.
- **Manufacturing plants**: fixed shifts = perfect match density, parking saturation, FMD budget (FR).
- **Multi-employer logistics parks**: one deployment covers many employers — white-label / park-operator angle.

## Next actions

1. Prioritize sites where the *park*, not the company, is the unit: Delta 3 (Dourges), DIRFT, Brackmills, Birch Coppice, ZIMEYSA (Satigny) — one deal covers many employers.
2. Buyer titles to find per company: FR "responsable RSE / mobilité", "DRH site"; CH "Mobilitätsmanager / responsable plan de mobilité"; UK "HR manager", "site/ops director", "sustainability manager".
3. Hooks: FR = FMD (600–900 €/an) + plan de mobilité employeur; GE (CH) = mandatory plans de mobilité; UK = ESG/recruitment radius + parking cost.
4. Not covered by this Maps sweep (target manually): hospitals (CHU Lyon Est, Northampton General…), Basel pharma campuses, Toulouse aerospace, and any Microsoft-365-heavy mid-size firm.
