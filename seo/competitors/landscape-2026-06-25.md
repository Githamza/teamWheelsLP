# Competitive Landscape — Corporate Carpooling Software — 2026-06-25

Market-level SEO read for TeamWheels across EN (US) and FR (France). Directional — buyer-intent
head terms are very low volume, so SERP composition matters more than traffic estimates.
TeamWheels' own position is anchored from GSC (first-party), not third-party estimates.

## Market read

- **This is two different markets.**
  - **FR is the real battleground** — corporate carpooling is a mature category with established
    players (Karos, BlaBlaCar Daily, Klaxit, Mobicoop, Comovee) and big adjacent volume.
  - **EN/US is thin** — buyer terms are tiny (10–70/mo) and easily won; a near-zero-authority site
    (Comovee, 9 keywords) ranks #1 for "carpooling software".
- **Market leaders:**
  - FR organic leader: **Karos** (karos.fr, ~11,400 est. traffic / 424 kw) — brand + domicile-travail.
  - FR consumer/B2B hybrid: **BlaBlaCar Daily** (owns Klaxit) — the discovery default.
  - Direct B2B-SaaS that wins the *commercial head term* in BOTH languages: **Comovee** (#1 for
    "logiciel covoiturage entreprise" FR and "carpooling software" EN — despite a tiny footprint).
  - EN-adjacent: **Wayleadr** (parking+commute, ~2,870 / 367 kw), **Scoop** (~1,257 / 240).
  - Informational SERPs owned by **publishers/gov**: bpifrance bigmedia ("9 applications" listicle),
    ecologie.gouv.fr, ADEME, observatoire.covoiturage.gouv.fr, pluxee.fr, generali.fr.
- **Most winnable opportunity:** the B2B-software intent is under-contested.
  - FR: **`covoiturage entreprise` (140/mo, KD 7, CPC €13)** and **`plateforme covoiturage entreprise`
    (30/mo, KD 12, CPC €21)** — TeamWheels is already pos 9–14; the new `/fr/` page can reach top 5.
  - FR scale prize: **`forfait mobilité durable` (6,600/mo, KD 20)** — by far the biggest winnable
    B2B-adjacent term; TeamWheels already has FMD content.
  - EN: the entire buyer cluster is **KD 18–21** — Comovee proves a small site can take #1.
- **Biggest barrier to ranking:** NOT difficulty or backlinks (KD is low; Comovee wins with 9
  keywords and no authority). The real barriers are:
  1. **Microscopic head-term volume** — ranking #1 for "carpool management software" (10/mo) yields
     almost nothing alone. Traffic requires capturing *adjacent/informational* volume (FMD, "best
     apps" listicles, ESG/Scope 3, how-to).
  2. **Listicle publishers own discovery** — bpifrance's "9 applications de covoiturage" ranks 3–4
     across every FR commercial query. Getting *into* those roundups (digital PR) beats out-ranking them.
  3. **Brand/navigational demand** goes to Karos and BlaBlaCar Daily.

## Content formats that are working

- **FR listicles** ("9 applications de covoiturage", "top 5 applications") — dominate commercial-
  discovery queries. App-centric (BlaBlaCar, Karos, Klaxit), **not** B2B-software comparisons.
- **Gov/institutional guides** (ADEME, ecologie.gouv, observatoire, INRS) — own informational + FMD.
- **Employer-benefit explainers** (pluxee, generali, seton, MMA) — "how to set up carpooling in your
  company" angle, ranking above some products.
- **Product /entreprises landing pages** (karos.fr/entreprises, blablacardaily.com/entreprises,
  comovee.com/fr) — the direct-competitor playbook TeamWheels' new pages now match.
- **EN:** sparse — product pages + Reddit/Quora threads + TDM/gov commute sites (511.org, county sites).

## Keyword / theme gaps (openings for TeamWheels)

1. **Microsoft Teams + carpooling — nobody targets it.** Every competitor is a standalone app. This
   is a defensible wedge against the universal *adoption-failure* pain. Build the "carpooling for
   Microsoft Teams / Microsoft 365" theme hard (already started with the new pages).
2. **FMD operational content** (`forfait mobilité durable`, 6,600/mo, KD 20) — how to fund/justify/
   report FMD via carpooling. Highest-volume winnable B2B-adjacent term; TeamWheels has the angle.
3. **B2B "best corporate carpooling software 2026" comparison** — the existing listicles are
   consumer-app roundups. A buyer-focused software comparison is unoccupied.
4. **Competitor-alternative pages** — "Karos alternative", "Klaxit / BlaBlaCar Daily for business
   alternative" — capture established-brand demand with the Teams differentiator.
5. **Scope 3 / CSRD employee commuting (EN)** — compliance angle barely contested; TeamWheels already
   has the scope-3 guide to build on.

## Backlink / authority observations

- Not the deciding factor here: Comovee ranks #1 for the head term with ~9 keywords and negligible
  authority, and commercial KD sits at 7–21. On-page relevance + a dedicated page wins these.
- Where authority *does* matter is the **informational/listicle layer** (bpifrance, gouv) — those are
  **digital-PR / link targets**, not pages to out-rank head-on. (Backlink API not queried — low ROI
  for this read given the low KD.)

  FR leader's footprint to mine keyword gaps).
