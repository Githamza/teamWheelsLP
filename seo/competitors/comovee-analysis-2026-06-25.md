# Competitor Analysis — Comovee (comovee.com) — 2026-06-25

Deep-dive on the direct B2B-SaaS competitor that ranks #1 for the commercial head term in both
EN and FR. Evidence: OpenSEO `get_ranked_keywords` (US), `get_domain_overview`, live FR SERPs from
the landscape. TeamWheels baseline from GSC. (Backlinks API disabled on account; per-keyword
backlink fields used instead.)

## Competitor snapshot

- **Footprint is tiny: 9 ranked keywords, ~81 est. monthly organic traffic, ~2 ranking URLs**
  (homepage + `/rideshare-software/`). No blog, no guides, no informational content.
- **Backlinks: ~9 referring domains / 13 backlinks to the homepage** (main_domain_rank 285). Negligible authority.
- Yet it ranks **#1 "carpooling for companies"**, **#2 "carpool software"**, **#2 "carpooling software"** (US),
  and **#1 "logiciel/outil covoiturage entreprise"** (FR live SERP) — all off a single optimized homepage.
- Positioning: *"the smartest, most intuitive all-in-one solution for companies to enable carpooling
  among their employees"* / *"the one corporate carpool system that simply works."* Standalone app — **no Teams**.

## Biggest lesson

**The head commercial terms are won by one razor-focused, exact-match B2B page in a low-competition
SERP — not by authority or content volume.** Comovee ranks #1–2 with 9 referring domains and zero
blog. The deciding factors are (1) an exact-match, company-focused homepage title/H1
("Carpooling **for Companies**"), and (2) almost no other dedicated B2B-software page existing to
compete. KD on these terms is 7–21. This is exactly the gap TeamWheels' new commercial page targets.

## Best opportunity to beat them

Comovee is **thin and stalling** — it captures the head term but *nothing around it*, and it's
already slipping ("carpooling software" dropped #1→#2). Two moves:
1. **Out-depth the head term.** TeamWheels' new `/en/corporate-carpooling-software/` already has far
   more than Comovee's one page (Teams differentiator, ROI, FAQ, internal-link mesh, 22 supporting
   blog posts). Match the exact-match clarity and TeamWheels should pass a 9-referring-domain page.
2. **Take everything Comovee ignores** — the adjacent/informational layer where it has zero presence.

## Content / page types working for them

- **One commercial homepage** doing all the work, hyper-optimized for "carpooling for companies /
  software." That's the entire strategy. Simple, and it works *because the SERP is empty of
  alternatives* — not because the page is strong.
- `/rideshare-software/` is their only other ranking page (barely, #69).
- No educational, comparison, calculator, or localized content.

## Backlink / authority notes

- ~9 referring domains / 13 backlinks to the ranking page; main_domain_rank 285. Authority is **not**
  why they win — proof the buyer cluster is winnable without a big link profile.
- Caveat: the broader terms they *fail* on ("carpool software" KD 47, "carpooling application" KD 41)
  have heavier link competition (avg 500+ / 42 ref domains in-SERP) — those need some authority to take.

## Head-to-head SERP observations

- **FR** (`logiciel covoiturage entreprise`): Comovee #1, **TeamWheels #9** — both on page 1; Comovee
  leads on exact-match homepage focus. TeamWheels' new `/fr/` page + FMD content should close this.
- **EN** (`corporate carpooling software` / `carpooling software`): Comovee #2 on homepage; TeamWheels
  was scattered (homepage ~pos 24) — the new dedicated page is the head-to-head play.
- TeamWheels already out-depths Comovee on content; the new pages remove the only edge Comovee had
  (a single focused commercial page).

## Priority actions

1. **Make the new commercial pages exact-match crisp** — ensure H1/title hit "corporate carpooling
   software" / "logiciel covoiturage entreprise" as cleanly as Comovee's "Carpooling for Companies."
   (Largely done — verify FR H1 leads with "Logiciel de Covoiturage Entreprise." It does.)
2. **Claim "carpooling application" (1,900/mo)** — the volume Comovee whiffs at #34. Needs a focused
   page/section + a few links. Highest-volume EN prize in the category.
3. **Widen the content moat** — keep building FMD / Scope 3 / "best corporate carpooling software"
   content Comovee will never have. This is durable; their single-page model can't follow.
4. **Light digital PR** (a handful of referring domains) would put TeamWheels' authority past
   Comovee's ~9 — decisive given how low the bar is.
5. **Counter-position on Microsoft Teams** in titles/snippets — the one thing Comovee structurally
   can't claim.

## What NOT to copy

- Don't copy the **single-page** strategy — it's why Comovee is capped at 9 keywords and slipping.
  TeamWheels' advantage is depth + the Teams wedge; lean into both.
